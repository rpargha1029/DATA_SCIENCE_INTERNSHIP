# Task 4: Netflix Titles — Insights Summary & "What Does This Mean?"

> **Note**: Place `netflix_titles.csv` in `data/` and run `python src/netflix_eda.py` to generate all 6 visualizations in `outputs/`.

## Key Findings & Interpretation

1. **Movie Dominance (~70% vs ~30% TV Shows)**
   - *What does this mean?* Netflix's catalog volume is historically anchored by feature films, though TV Shows drive longer multi-episode engagement and subscriber retention.
2. **Rapid Catalog Expansion Post-2015**
   - *What does this mean?* Content releases surged sharply between 2015 and 2020 as Netflix scaled original productions globally before stabilizing.
3. **Top Genres: International Movies, Dramas, and Comedies**
   - *What does this mean?* Multi-label genre tagging shows strong investment in localized international cinema alongside universally popular Drama and Comedy categories.
4. **Top 5 Content-Producing Countries**
   - *What does this mean?* The United States and India lead total title volume, followed by the UK, Japan, and South Korea—reflecting key regional production hubs.
5. **Bonus 2-Year Trend Forecast**
   - *What does this mean?* A linear regression trend fit on recent historical release counts projects continued high-volume output, while highlighting that pandemic-era production dips can skew short-window linear extrapolations.
