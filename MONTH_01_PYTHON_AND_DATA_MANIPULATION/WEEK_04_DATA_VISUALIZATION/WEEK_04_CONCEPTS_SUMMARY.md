# Week 4 Concepts Summary: Data Visualization with Matplotlib and Seaborn

## Topics Covered
1. **Matplotlib Fundamentals**: Creating figures/axes (`plt.subplots`), customizing titles, labels, layouts, and exporting high-DPI PNGs.
2. **Seaborn Statistical Plots**:
   - `histplot` with KDE for univariate distributions.
   - `scatterplot` with `hue` grouping for bivariate relationships.
   - `heatmap` for correlation matrices.
   - `pairplot` for multi-feature pairwise exploration.
   - `barplot` for categorical comparisons.
3. **Multi-Panel Dashboard**: Combining 4 complementary views into a single executive dashboard figure.

## Deliverables Implemented
- `visualization_dashboard.py`: Generates dataset (`data/employee_performance.csv`) and saves 5 PNG plots in `outputs/` (`01_sales_histogram.png`, `02_experience_vs_sales_scatter.png`, `03_correlation_heatmap.png`, `04_feature_pairplot.png`, `05_client_visualization_dashboard.png`).
